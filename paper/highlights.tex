\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Human task scheduling is cast as a POMDP with latent attention and fatigue.
\item Rest breaks are explicit actions of memory-augmented DQN and PPO schedulers.
\item Tuned rest rules are strong; the PPO scheduler beats the best of nine rules.
\item Learned policies alone protect hard tasks while maximising on-time completion.
\item Results hold under sensor noise and $\pm$30\% changes of all cognitive parameters.
\end{itemize}
