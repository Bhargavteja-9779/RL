\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Human task scheduling is cast as a POMDP with latent attention and fatigue.
\item Rest breaks are explicit actions chosen by a memory-augmented deep Q-network.
\item Agent beats tuned break heuristics: \aaOnTimePct\% vs \bestHeurOnTimePct\% of tasks on time.
\item The learned policy rests during the post-lunch dip and does hard tasks early.
\item Ablations show the gain depends on modelling dynamic human cognition.
\end{itemize}
