\noindent\textbf{Symbols}\par\smallskip
\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.27\linewidth}p{0.70\linewidth}@{}}
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Workload and time}} \\
$t$, $T$, $\Delta$ & decision slot index, slots per day ($T=48$), slot length ($\Delta=10$ min) \\
$h_t$ & clock time of slot $t$ in hours, $h_t=9+t\Delta/60$ \\
$N$ & number of tasks per day \\
$e_i$, $d_i$, $w_i$, $D_i$ & arrival (entry) slot, difficulty, work content and deadline of task $i$ \\
$s_i$ & relative deadline slack of task $i$ \\
$R_{i,t}$, $\tau_i$ & remaining work and completion slot of task $i$ \\
$\mathcal P_t$, $\mathcal E$, $\mathcal H$ & available unfinished tasks; easy tasks; hard tasks \\
$\mathcal C_t$, $\mathcal M_t$ & tasks completed / deadlines missed in slot $t$ \\
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Cognitive state and observation}} \\
$H_t$ & homeostatic attention resource (latent) \\
$A_t$, $c(h)$ & effective attention (latent); circadian modulation \\
$F_t$ & fatigue (latent) \\
$\rho_t$, $\rho_0$ & work rate (work units per slot); work rate of a fully rested worker \\
$\alpha$, $\phi$, $\beta$ & attention depletion rate, fatigue coupling, attention recovery rate \\
$\gamma$ & circadian amplitude \\
$\delta$, $\eta$, $\kappa$ & fatigue accumulation rate, fatigue recovery rate, fatigue throughput penalty \\
$\sigma_p$, $\xi_t$ & process-noise scale and standard normal process noise \\
$\sigma_o$, $\epsilon_t$ & observation-noise scale and standard normal observation noise \\
$\tilde A_t$, $\tilde F_t$ & noisy observations of attention and fatigue \\
$u_t$, $b_t$ & indicators of working and resting in slot $t$ \\
$o_t$, $x_t$, $k$ & observation vector, observation history, history length \\
$n_t$, $W_t$, $\sigma_t$, $m_t$, $\omega_t$ & per-class queue length, remaining work, normalised slack, overdue count, shortest remaining work \\
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Decision problem and learning}} \\
$\mathcal S$, $\mathcal A$, $\Omega$ & state, action and observation spaces \\
$P$, $O$, $R$ & transition, observation and reward functions \\
$a_t$, $g(\cdot)$ & macro-action and dispatch function \\
$r_t$, $\lambda$, $\mu$ & reward; fatigue and attention weights \\
$\pi$, $J(\pi)$, $\mathcal B_t$ & policy, expected return, belief state \\
$\gamma_{\mathrm{RL}}$ & discount factor (distinct from the circadian amplitude $\gamma$) \\
$Q$, $V$, $\mathrm{Adv}$ & action-value, state-value and advantage functions \\
$\theta$, $\bar\theta$ & online and target network parameters \\
$y_t$, $\mathcal L$, $\ell_\delta$ & TD target, loss, Huber function \\
$\mathcal D$ & replay buffer \\
$\epsilon_n$ & exploration rate after $n$ environment steps \\
$\pi_\theta$, $V_\psi$ & PPO policy and value networks \\
$\varrho_t$, $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{c}}$ & PPO probability ratio and clipping parameter \\
$\widehat{\mathrm{Adv}}_t$, $\hat G_t$ & generalised advantage estimate and return target \\
$c_v$, $c_e$, $\mathbb H$ & value and entropy coefficients; entropy \\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{GAE}}$ & GAE parameter \\
$\eta_{\mathrm{lr}}$ & learning rate \\
$r_{rb}$ & matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation \\
\end{tabular}
